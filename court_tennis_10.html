Sets or Games?
Sets — games count toward sets (first to 6 games, best of 3 sets).
Games — running total of games won only, no sets.
Step 4 of 6
Game Ball or Deuce?
Game Ball — first to win 4 points wins the game: 0 → 15 → 30 → 40 → game. At 40-40, whoever wins the next point wins the game. No advantage.

Deuce — first to 4 points, but if both reach 40 together, a player must then win 2 consecutive points (advantage, then game). Losing the advantage point returns to deuce.
